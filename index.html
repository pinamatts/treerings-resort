Good to know

Before you book.

Booking and payment

Fifty per cent is paid on check-in, with a valid ID. A ₱1,000 cash security deposit is collected and returned in full when you leave.

Rates may change with demand and availability, and holidays can differ.

Check-in and check-out

Rooms: check in from 2 PM, check out at 12 noon. Exclusive and whole-house bookings check out at 11 AM.

Day use runs 8 AM to 5 PM or 1 PM to 8 PM, nine hours at most, with a strict 8 PM close. Extra hours are ₱1,000 each.

Extra guests and children

Rooms: extra adult ₱500, twelve and under ₱250, seven and under free, up to two per room.

Day use: extra adult ₱250, twelve and under ₱100, seven and under free, up to four children.

Exclusive use: extra adult ₱500, twelve and under ₱250, seven and under ₱250. Children four to seven come free, up to four.

Evening hours

Beach access closes at 10 PM. Gates and pool lights close at 10:30 PM, and karaoke volume comes down at the same time. We sit in a residential area, so this one matters.

In the pool

Proper swimming attire only. No jewellery, and no clothing with buttons. There is no lifeguard, so children need watching.

Food and drink

No corkage fee, so bring what you like. The kitchen, utensils, refrigerator, griller and smoker are yours to use, along with a gallon of drinking water.

Breakfast is not included with rooms.
